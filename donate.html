Support the department

Your gift stays in Bluff City

Bluff City Volunteer Fire Department is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Every gift helps pay for protective gear, truck maintenance, training, and station improvements as we approach our 100th year.

Thank you to every neighbor, business, and partner who has stood behind us.

Ways to give

  • Mail a check payable to Bluff City Volunteer Fire Department, PO Box 366, Bluff City, TN 37618
  • Bring a gift to our Tuesday night meeting at 7 PM, 4256 Bluff City Highway
  • Call 423-538-5221 to arrange a gift
  • Buy from our fundraisers

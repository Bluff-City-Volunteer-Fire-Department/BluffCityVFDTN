Volunteers wanted

Answer the call.

Our department depends on volunteers who dedicate their time to protecting the community. You do not need experience to start. We train new members, and there are roles beyond the fire ground too, including support, fundraising, and public education.

What to expect

Ask about training at any Tuesday meeting.

Who can apply

Ask at any Tuesday meeting.

How to start

Come to any Tuesday night meeting at 7 PM at 4256 Bluff City Highway. You can pick up an application and meet our members.

Or call 423-538-5221.

Volunteer application

Tell us a little about yourself and a department member will contact you about next steps. You are also welcome to come to any Tuesday night meeting at 7 PM.
